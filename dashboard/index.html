Today's home visits

CareOps reads family messages in English and Hinglish, drafts the reply, and suggests a fix. You approve; nothing is booked or sent on its own.

Approving shows what would happen next. In this demo nothing is sent or booked.
About the risk model and its known limitation The list is sized to what one coordinator can review: the top ~10% of today's visits by predicted disruption risk are "Likely to go wrong", the next ~10% "Worth a check", the same capacity assumption used in this project's economics, fairness and intervention-simulation modules. Predictions come from a logistic regression on 6 features (ROC-AUC 0.58 after a data-leakage fix), so treat it as a "look here first" helper, not a verdict. Suggested actions are rule-based. This is one representative simulated day, not a cherry-picked best case.

Known limitation: first-time-patient predictions are overconfident relative to observed disruption in this small subgroup — mean predicted risk 25.6% against an actual disruption rate of 17.0% (a +8.6-point calibration gap, vs. +1.3 points for returning patients), on a subgroup 66x smaller than the returning-patient one. This is a calibration issue, not a flagging-rate issue: the subgroup check in fairness_subgroup_analysis.py found first-time patients are actually flagged less often than returning patients (10.6% vs 20.1%) and have a lower false-positive rate (10.3% vs 18.0%). Calibration should be reviewed before any production use. Names, times and phone numbers are synthetic.
Visit reports
After a nursing or companion visit, the caregiver sends a voice note. CareOps turns it into a report and a family update. Checks after the AI decide what is trusted: a number must actually appear in the note, red flags (a fall, very low sugar) always go to you, and the family update waits until you have looked.
Try your own note
On this public demo the AI is switched off, so every note goes to you for review. The readings and red-flag checks still run. The recorded examples above show the full pipeline.
Synthetic notes · not clinical guidance · thresholds are illustrative routing rules that only decide whether a person looks first. To see live AI calls, run it locally with a free Groq key.
Fix a visit
A nurse can't make it. Pick the visit and CareOps finds the best replacement, with its reasons, and drafts the message to the family.
