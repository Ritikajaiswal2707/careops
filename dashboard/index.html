How it was built

Today's home visits

CareOps reads family messages in English and Hinglish, drafts the reply, and suggests a fix. You approve; nothing is booked or sent on its own.

Where AI is used, and why it is off here
  • AI Visit reports: an LLM (gpt-oss-120b) turns a caregiver's note into a report and a family update. Checks after the AI decide what is trusted.
  • AI Family messages: rules read them first; an LLM is asked only when the rules can't decide. Every message on today's list was read by rules.
  • Model "Act first" and "Check today": a simple statistical model (logistic regression), not generative AI.
  • Rules Replacement nurse: hard eligibility checks, then a weighted score with reasons.
  • Template Messages to families: fixed templates you edit, so the wording is predictable.

Why the live AI is off on this page: it is a free public demo, so it has no paid API key and never sends text to an outside model. The visit-report examples are recorded from a real AI run. Run it locally with a free Groq key to see live calls.

Approving shows what would happen next. In this demo nothing is sent or booked.
About the risk model and its known limitation The list is sized to what one coordinator can review in a day: the riskiest 10% of visits are marked "Act first" and the next 10% "Check today". The risk score is a simple statistical model (ROC-AUC 0.58), so it is a "look here first" helper, not a verdict.

Known limitation: for first-time patients the model predicts more risk than actually happens (25.6% predicted, 17.0% observed), so its scores for them should be recalibrated before any real use.
Visit reports
After a nursing or companion visit, the caregiver sends a voice note. CareOps turns it into a report and a family update. Checks after the AI decide what is trusted: a number must actually appear in the note, red flags (a fall, very low sugar) always go to you, and the family update waits until you have looked.
Try your own note
The live AI is off on this public demo, so your note goes to a person for review. The readings and red-flag checks still run.
Thresholds only decide whether a person looks first. They follow ACC/AHA, ADA and NEWS2 lines and are not clinically validated (sources).
A nurse is out today
When a nurse calls in sick, all her visits need cover. CareOps checks each one and suggests who can take it, without giving one nurse more visits than she has room for.
Fix a visit
A nurse can't make one visit. Pick it and CareOps finds the best replacement, with its reasons, and drafts the message to the family.
